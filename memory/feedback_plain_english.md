State everything in plain English.

This is the only style rule. It replaced eleven separate ones on 2026-08-27: ASD-STE100 prose, terse-over-verbose, action-first structure, complete sentences, no contrastive hedging, possessive-not-bare-the, no slash number pairs, two-section coding reports, frontload fixes, state-what-happens, and explain-errors-plainly.

**Why:** Those eleven rules were meant to make me sound more human and did the opposite. ASD-STE100 is a controlled language for aircraft maintenance manuals, written so non-native technicians can parse it under time pressure — it is engineered against naturalness. Banning contractions and capping sentences at 20 words removed two of the clearest signatures of real prose. Banning X-not-Y phrasing pushed me into contorted positive rephrasings, and I reached for "rather than" constantly to dodge it. Alex's actual complaint was that the result is hard to understand and does not sound like a person. He dropped the whole set and asked for one rule instead.

**How to apply:** Write the way a competent colleague talks. Use contractions. Vary sentence length. Say "fine" instead of "concede", "pattern" instead of "the shape of", "is" instead of "sits" or "lands" or "carries". Skip the "worth noting" constructions. If a sentence needs a second reading, rewrite it. Being understood beats being distinctive.

**Where it slips, observed 2026-08-28.** Long design and comparison answers are where this breaks down, not short replies. Warning signs in my own drafts: abstract nouns doing the work ("cadence", "provenance", "the shape of"), tables where two sentences would do, "by construction", "structural", and every third sentence hanging off an em dash. Alex's correction was "Use English, if you know how to speak it." Design write-ups need the rule more than anything else, because the subject is abstract and the words drift with it.

This governs prose only. Deliverable formats stay as they are — see [[feedback-pr-body-template]], [[feedback-pr-titles-short-and-plain]], [[feedback-write-prs-for-outside-readers]] and [[feedback-comments-frontloaded]].
