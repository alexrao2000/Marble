Write prose in ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English. The rules do not apply to code, identifiers, or command syntax. They also do not apply to marketing copy or essays, because STE removes voice on purpose.

**Why:** the user gave the six rules directly as a standing instruction on 2026-08-07, and asked to keep them. STE is the register the user wants for findings, PR bodies, issues, and status lines.

**How to apply:** check each sentence before you send it.
1. Sentence over 20 words - split it.
2. Semicolon - replace with a period.
3. Contraction - expand it.
4. Passive voice with a known actor - make it active.
5. "-ing" main verb, nominalization ("perform an analysis"), or phrasal verb ("spin up") - use a plain verb.
6. Same thing named two ways - pick one name.

This is the sentence-level rule. [[feedback-action-first-structure]] is the message-level rule, and both sit under [[feedback-terse-over-verbose]]. Rule 4 and rule 6 also serve [[feedback-write-prs-for-outside-readers]]. Rule 6 overlaps [[feedback-no-contrastive-hedging]].
