For product presentations, show solid examples from the actual product using populated,
synthetic demo data. A feature table or generic illustration does not replace a useful
product screenshot. Label examples honestly and do not imply measured customer results.

Design proportions and alignment deliberately. Establish a consistent content grid,
outer margins, typography hierarchy and spacing. Let the main screenshot dominate its
slide when it carries the evidence. Preserve image aspect ratios, crop to a useful focal
area, and keep related edges and baselines aligned. Cut copy before shrinking it.

Inspect every final slide at presentation size and review the overall sequence. Check
that screenshot content remains legible, important elements are not cropped, and images
persist in the saved deliverable. Local rendering alone does not establish that the
user's viewer can load an image. Embed portable raster assets where appropriate, and
state any viewer check that could not be completed.

This feedback followed a deck with a broken image, too little product evidence and weak
proportions and alignment. Treat these as acceptance criteria for future visual work.
