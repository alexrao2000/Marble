Default to short, direct statements over long explanatory paragraphs. When a finding, a decision, or a status update can be a few bullet points or short sentences, use that instead of dense prose.

**Why:** user explicitly compared a terse legal/government document favorably against typical response style, calling long-form explanation "shit you spew out on a daily basis." This is a strong, direct signal that verbosity itself is the problem, independent of correctness.

**How to apply:** [[feedback-ste-prose]] gives the sentence-level rules and [[feedback-action-first-structure]] gives the message-level shape. Related to [[feedback_no_contrastive_hedging]] and [[feedback_write_prs_for_outside_readers]] (both about cutting register/hedging), but broader — this is about overall length and density, not just phrasing style. Before sending a response, cut restating context the user already has, cut multi-clause justification sentences, and prefer short declarative lines or bullets. Save the long-form investigation narration for when the user asks "why" or "walk me through it" — default to the short version otherwise.
