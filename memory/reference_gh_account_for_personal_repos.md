metadata:

An active enterprise account may be unable to write to a personal repository even when
git pushes succeed. Use the stored personal account credential for the command instead
of switching the active account and disturbing another session.

The personal account is `alexrao2000`. Directory-scoped account settings may not cover
a worktree at another path; check its account before writing.
